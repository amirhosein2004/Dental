{% comment %} Both practices, on every page — address, numbers and opening hours together in one block per city. Repeating the name, address and phone of each location site-wide is the thing that ties the website to each Business Profile listing: they are compared for an exact match, and a footer that named only one city left the other unattached to its own site. Hours sit inside the branch block rather than in a column of their own. One shared "ساعات کاری" column could only ever be right about one of the two cities, and a visitor had no way to tell which one it was describing. `branches` comes from about.context_processors and is cached, so this costs no query. {% endcomment %}
