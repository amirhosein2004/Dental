{% comment %} The site's two ways to reach the clinic, in one place. Every page previously ended with its own call-to-action block, all of them pointing at the contact form with different wording — so "رزرو نوبت" led to a message form, and the phone number was nowhere near the button. A visitor had to work out which page did what. Now every page includes this partial and offers the same two actions in the same order: book online, then get in touch. "تماس با ما" is a link to the contact page, not a number and not a menu of numbers. There is no clinic-wide line: the practice has one number per city, so a button printing a single one of them said nothing about where it rang — a visitor in Quchan pressed it and reached Mashhad — and a dropdown asked the visitor to choose a city from nothing but two phone numbers. The contact page already answers that: each practice with its own address, hours, map, numbers and the message form, all on one screen.
